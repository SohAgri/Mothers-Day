My Letter To You, Mom

Dear Mom,

I have watched you turn ordinary days into safe homes for me. You noticed when I was silent, understood when I could not explain, and stood beside me when life felt heavy.

You sacrificed your comfort for my happiness. You stayed strong even during difficult days. Through every fever, every exam night, every fear I hid behind a smile, your care never paused.

I still remember childhood moments where your hand on my head made everything feel okay. In the hardest times, your words gave me courage to continue.

I may not say it every day, but I love you Mom. Thank you for your patience, your strength, and the kind of love that asks for nothing and gives everything.

With all my heart,
Sohum
